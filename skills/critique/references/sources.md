## Misreadings (do not propagate — detail in `docs/research/hci-ux-foundations.md` §6)
"7±2 items per screen" (Miller is about recall, not visible items; ~4 chunks today) · the 3-click rule (no data) ·
Fitts as "make it big" (distance matters; dangerous targets harder) · "affordance" for every cue (signifiers on
screens) · the F-pattern as a goal (it is a symptom) · "always confirm" (undo beats habituated dialogs) · one right
OK/Cancel order (platform + consistency) · "5 seconds" as a law (a convention) · SUS 70 = 70th percentile (68 is average).
